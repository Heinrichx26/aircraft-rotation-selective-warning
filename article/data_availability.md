The supplementary archive in the [public release](https://github.com/Heinrichx26/aircraft-rotation-selective-warning/releases/tag/reproducibility-v1.2) contains code and reference results for flight connection reconstruction and temporal model evaluation on the January 2008 United States Airline On-Time Data. It includes archive integrity checks, the model and threshold settings, simultaneous policy-grid bounds, and constructed examples for assignment changes and matched-policy evaluation. Access to the domestic operational archive is governed by aviation security, confidentiality and institutional approval requirements. The article reports its cohort definitions, temporal partitions, model settings and aggregate results.
