Access to domestic flight-operation records for model fitting and evaluation is governed by aviation-security, confidentiality and institutional-approval requirements. The article reports cohort definitions, temporal partitions, model settings, selection grids and aggregate results; supplementary tables, model details and sensitivity analyses provide supporting evidence. Source code and reference outputs are available in the Supplementary Material archive and the companion public repository release (reproducibility-v1.3: [public release page](https://github.com/Heinrichx26/aircraft-rotation-selective-warning/releases/tag/reproducibility-v1.3)). The release downloads and verifies the January 2008 United States airline on-time archive, reconstructs flight connections and applies the selection and screening procedure to all 21 budgets. Checksummed reference outputs, simultaneous policy-grid limits and executable assignment-event, paired-comparison and workload-comparison examples accompany the code. The release and the Supplementary Material archive also contain the article pipeline modules, a model card with the calibration and gate coefficients, the complete candidate grid with its selection-period counts, a field schema, an end-to-end run on a constructed calendar and the article tables as comma-separated value files.
